You need weather data in production. Not a research project. Not a 40 tab vendor evaluation. A real decision you can explain in Slack, put in an architecture decision record, and move on from. TLDR verdict For most production apps, pick Weatherstack . It is the cleaner choice for current weather, historical lookups, simple forecasts, and fast REST API integration.
